How this works
  1. The host shows a sentence with a blank, here and on the big screen.
  2. When the timer starts, type one word to fill the blank. You have 10 seconds.
  3. Meanwhile the machine fills the same blank 20 times.
  4. Be the machine: score by how often the machine picks your word. Beat the machine: score 100 for a word that neither the machine nor anyone else said.
  5. Temperature controls how adventurous the machine is. Watch what happens as it rises.
