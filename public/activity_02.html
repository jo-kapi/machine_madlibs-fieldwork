How this works
  1. Type a theme and press Generate Words. Leave it blank for filler words like "the" and "of".
  2. Click a word to put it on the canvas. Click it again to take it off.
  3. Drag words around. Select one on the canvas to restyle it.
  4. Colours can be hex codes (#ff3b5b) or a description ("burnt orange"). The machine turns descriptions into colours.
  5. Download your image when you're done.
